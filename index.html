Travel

At Gomo Adventures, we believe travel is more than visiting new places—it’s about creating meaningful connections, discovering authentic cultures, and making memories that last a lifetime.

Since 2008, we have welcomed travelers to Bhutan with genuine hospitality, local expertise, and thoughtfully crafted journeys that reveal the true heart and soul of our country.

Every journey is designed to inspire a deeper appreciation of Bhutan while supporting responsible tourism and preserving our unique cultural and natural heritage.

We look forward to welcoming you to Bhutan—not just as visitors, but as our honored guests.

Ugyen Wangchuk (Punap)

Guiding is not just our profession; it is our passion.

Why travel with Gomo Adventures?

We believe that travel should be personal, meaningful, and unforgettable. Led by experienced tour guide Punap Ugyen Wangchuk, a local travel professional, and supported by a dedicated network of trusted partners, we specialize in crafting tailor-made journeys that showcase the very best of Bhutan.

Whether you seek cultural discovery, spiritual enrichment, breathtaking landscapes, nature based travel, photography opportunities, or trekking adventures, every itinerary is thoughtfully designed around your interests and travel style. Our deep local knowledge, attention to detail, and commitment to genuine Bhutanese hospitality ensure a seamless and stress-free experience from the moment you arrive.

More than just a tour operator, we are passionate storytellers and guides who take pride in creating authentic connections between travelers and the rich culture, traditions, and natural beauty of Bhutan.
