Plan Your Journey

“I was nineteen when I met a photographer walking through my country.”

He was shooting for a magazine, and something in it caught me. Years later, studying in America, a friend took me along on his shoots, taught me what he knew, and gave me my first camera. I brought it home.

Since 2002 I have guided travellers through Bhutan — its dzongs and high passes, its festivals and farm kitchens — and in 2008 I founded Gomo Adventures to do it my own way: small journeys, planned personally, at an unhurried pace. I am from Punakha, which is why people here call me Punap. I photograph this country, I write about it, and I am still learning from it.

Come and see it with me — not as a visitor, but as an honoured guest.

Ugyen Wangchuk (Punap)

Founder & Lead Guide
