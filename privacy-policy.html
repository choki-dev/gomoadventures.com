Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated — July 2026

GOMO Adventures respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you enquire about or book a journey with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a proposal, or book a trip, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, passport details, dietary or medical information relevant to your trip, and payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare trip proposals.
  • To arrange visas, permits, accommodation, guides, and transportation on your behalf.
  • To communicate with you before, during, and after your trip.
  • To send occasional travel tips, festival dates, or seasonal offers, if you opt in.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share the minimum information necessary with the partners who deliver your trip — local guides, lodges, and the Bhutanese authorities responsible for visas and permits. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Payment details are handled through secure channels and are not stored on our own systems any longer than necessary.

5. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe from our updates. To do so, please get in touch.
