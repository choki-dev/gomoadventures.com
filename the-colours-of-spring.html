Trip Overview

Bhutan Through the Lens

Bhutan’s spring time unfolds like a breathtaking tapestry. Imagine waking to clear mornings, the air abuzz with warmth and gentle rain. A vibrant sea of blooming flowers stretches as far as the eyes can see, with magnolias and rhododendrons exploding in color, creating a scene fit for a postcard. Our exclusive tour whisks you through central Bhutan, where you’ll be captivated by the endless beauty and warm hospitality of rural communities.

Immerse yourself in cultural programs, traditional games, and exquisite local crafts. Every corner is a feast for the senses, from the mouthwatering cuisine to the vibrant artistic traditions. As you venture through the countryside, witness the rhythmic dance of farmers preparing their fields for spring planting, adding a touch of authenticity to your experience. This unforgettable journey promises an escape into a world of unparalleled natural wonder and rich cultural heritage. Join us on this extraordinary adventure and create memories that will forever be etched in your heart.

Itinerary

Day 1 Paro

Arrive in Bhutan from Bangkok, Thailand. After arrival in Paro, drive to Thimphu, the capital city of Bhutan. Visit Dechen Phodrang Central Monastic Body. In the evening, explore Thimphu city.

Day 2 Thimphu

In the morning, photograph the tallest sitting Buddha, the vegetable market and archery, and visit the Textile Museum. After lunch, visit the Royal Academy of Performing Arts and Simtokha Dzong to photograph monks studying and debating, and many more.

Day 3 Punakha

Early in the morning we drive to Punakha and stop at Dochula (10,000 feet) for morning-light photography of the mountains and beautiful chortens, with breakfast and a Himalayan view. Drive down to Lobesa village for photographs and visit the temple of the Divine Madman. In the evening, visit Punakha Dzong for night photography, and many more.

Day 4 Punakha

Drive to Talo to witness the festival and photograph the mask dances and folk dances along with colorful locals, and many more.

Day 5 Punakha / Wangdue

After breakfast, visit Nezergang Domchey (a local festival) for photography and a cultural event. In the evening, visit Wangdue Phodrang Dzong for more evening photography, and many more.

Day 6 Wangdue / Phobjikha

Visit the Shange local festival for photography and a rural countryside experience, with a farmhouse visit and an authentic meal, and many more.

Day 7 Trongsa / Bumthang

After breakfast we drive to the Bumthang valley, stopping for lunch and coffee at the Waterfall Cafe in Trongsa, and many more.

Day 8 Bumthang

Explore the Chamkhar valley and take photographs around it. In the afternoon, drive to Tang via the nunnery and the Burning Lake, and many more.

Day 9 Bumthang

Visit the Tang Ugyen Choling heritage museum and rest. In the evening, walk around and take photographs, and many more.

Day 10 Phobjikha

Drive back to Phobjikha via Chendebji Chorten with a lunch break. On arrival at Gangtey, visit Gangtey Monastery and hike down to the Phobjikha valley, and many more.

Day 11 Paro

In the morning we drive back to the Paro valley and enjoy the scenic drive. On arrival, visit Paro Dzong, with an evening ceremony and photography, and many more.

Day 12 Paro / Tiger’s Nest

In the morning, visit Dzongdrakha Monastery for photography, followed by the hike to the iconic Tiger’s Nest Monastery. In the evening we can explore Paro town.

Day 13 Departure

Departure to Bangkok, Delhi, Dhaka, Kathmandu or Singapore.
