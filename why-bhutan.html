WHY BHUTAN?

Indeed, Bhutan stands as one of the few countries in the world that has never been colonized.

Throughout its history, Bhutan has maintained its sovereignty and independence, preserving its unique culture, traditions, and way of life. This absence of colonization has played a significant role in shaping Bhutan's distinct identity and contributing to its exceptional status in the world.
