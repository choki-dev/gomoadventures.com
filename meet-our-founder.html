Meet the Founder

Punap Ugyen Wangchuk

Punap Ugyen Wangchuk, popularly Punap Ugyen, is a monk-turned-veteran tour guide, versatile storyteller, photographer, author, and environmental enthusiast. Following a 13-year stint as a monk, he founded Gomo Adventure, a company specializing in culture, photography, adventure, and eco-friendly tours.

He has authored books on children, photography, and Bhutanese cuisine, and designed the celebrated Wangdue Eco-Lodge in his quest to showcase traditional architecture and promote eco-friendly travel. Learn more about his exciting 20-year experience in the Bhutanese tourism industry.
