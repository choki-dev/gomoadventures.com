Trip Overview

Bhutan Live Art Tour

Indulge in the stunning landscapes of Bhutan, featuring awe-inspiring mountains and wild rivers flowing towards India. Adorned with picturesque temples and monasteries, the country is a masterpiece waiting to be captured on canvas. Our tour offers you a unique opportunity to explore these scenic locations with art materials provided, allowing you to create your own masterpiece while being inspired by the beauty of the mountains, valleys, and vistas.

You will be accompanied by local artists who will share insights into Bhutan’s rich art culture, including traditional Thangka paintings and monastic wall paintings, as well as the natural pigments used in these artworks. Meet renowned Bhutanese artists and explore local art galleries to learn more about the country’s art scene.

Experience the creation of a sacred sand mandala, a temporary work of art that carries profound meaning and teaches the impermanence of life. Witnessing the creation of a mandala is a unique and unforgettable blessing. Join us on this tour to discover the splendor of Bhutan and its unique art culture, and capture it on canvas like a true artist.

Plan This Journey

Trip Highlights

  • Enjoying beautiful scenery with live painting opportunities
  • Witnessing the creation of a sand mandala and photographing it
  • Meeting local traditional painters and contemporary artists to exchange knowledge
  • Hiking to iconic monasteries and temples, and a chanting experience
  • Tea with monks, a farmhouse visit and authentic meals, and many more

Itinerary

Day 1 Paro

Arrive in Paro. Depending on flight timing, we either drive to Thimphu or visit Paro Dzong after arrival.

Day 2 Thimphu

In the morning, visit the tallest Buddha and Thimphu’s main traffic point — an opportunity now, as it may not be there in the future. Live painting and breakfast at Dechen Phodrang. In the afternoon, visit Tashichho Dzong, then relax in the evening.

Day 3 Thimphu

Drive to Dodena and hike to Tango Monastery, with live painting at Tango Nye, the holy cave, and the rock cliff of Tandin (the deity of the horse-headed sadhana). After lunch, live painting of Cheri Monastery with the Wangchhu river in the foreground, then visit Tango Tashithang university and attend evening prayer.

Day 4 Punakha

Drive to Talo to witness the festival and photograph the mask dances and folk dances along with colorful locals, and many more.

Day 5 Punakha

Morning drive to Punakha Dzong for live painting of the dzong from different angles. Visit Yebesa village and the Jawana homestay for painting and a cultural experience.

Day 6 Punakha

Stay at Dumra Farm Resort for live painting of Punakha Dzong with its two rivers, male and female. In the afternoon, drive to Lumukha village for live painting and a homestay experience with a hot stone bath.

Day 7 Wangdue

Drive to Wangdue Phodrang, or hike down to Mendagang and the longest suspension bridge in Punakha, then on to Wangdue Phodrang for an overnight stay.

Day 8 Wangdue / Phobjikha

Morning live painting of Ngashigaykha village with Wangdue Phodrang Dzong, then move to the Phobjikha valley, stopping at Nobding on the way for lunch and painting.

Day 9 Phobjikha

In the Phobjikha valley, visit Gangtey Monastery and take a nature hike, with live painting of Gangtey Monastery, the village and the valley.

Day 10 Paro

Drive back to Paro and paint Paro Dzong with its traditional bridge.

Day 11 Paro / Tiger’s Nest

Hike to Tiger’s Nest, with live painting of the monastery.

Day 12 Departure

Departure to Bangkok, Delhi, Dhaka, Kathmandu or Singapore.
