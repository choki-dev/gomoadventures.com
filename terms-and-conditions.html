Terms & Conditions

The terms that govern your booking and travel with GOMO Adventures

Last updated — July 2026

These terms and conditions apply to every booking made with GOMO Adventures, whether for a scheduled small-group journey or a fully private, custom-built itinerary. By confirming a booking with us, you agree to the terms set out below.

1. Booking & Payment

A booking is confirmed once we receive a signed booking form and the required deposit. Because all travel to Bhutan is arranged in advance with our local partners — guides, lodges, and permits — deposits secure your dates and cannot be treated as provisional.

The remaining balance is due ahead of your departure date, as specified in your individual trip proposal. Payments can be made by bank transfer or another method agreed with our team.

2. Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellations must be submitted in writing. Because permits, lodges and guides are booked and paid for ahead of your trip, cancellation fees apply on a sliding scale based on how close to departure the cancellation is made. Exact terms are confirmed at the time of booking and set out in your trip proposal.

We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance that covers trip cancellation, interruption, and medical emergencies.

3. Your Responsibilities

  • Hold a valid passport with at least six months' validity beyond your travel dates.
  • Obtain the visa and permits required for entry to Bhutan, which our team assists with.
  • Disclose any medical conditions relevant to trekking, altitude, or physical activity in advance.
  • Respect local customs, dress codes, and guidance given at religious and cultural sites.

4. Changes to Itineraries

Bhutan's mountain roads, weather, and festival calendar can shift with little notice. We reserve the right to adjust an itinerary where necessary for safety or practicality, and will always aim to offer an equivalent alternative. Where a change is significant, we will discuss it with you first.

5. Liability

GOMO Adventures takes care in selecting guides, drivers, and accommodation partners, but cannot be held liable for loss, injury, delay, or expense arising from circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including weather, road conditions, or acts of third parties.

6. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Bhutan. If you have any questions about these terms, please contact our team before confirming your booking.
