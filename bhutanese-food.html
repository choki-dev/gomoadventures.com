Bhutanese Foods

A Taste of the Himalayan Kingdom

A Culinary Adventure

Bhutanese Cuisine

Bhutanese cuisine is a rich tapestry woven from cultural traditions, local ingredients, and a unique blend of flavors. It's a reflection of Bhutan's distinct culture and way of life, deeply intertwined with the country's history and geography. Travelers to Bhutan can expect a culinary adventure that complements the country's cultural experiences.

01

Ema Datshi

Signature Dish

This iconic dish consists of fiery chili peppers and Bhutanese cheese, creating a spicy and creamy concoction that's a staple in Bhutanese meals.

02

Red Rice

Staple Grain

Red rice, a nutrient-rich grain, is the dietary foundation. Its nutty flavor and distinct color contribute to many Bhutanese dishes.

03

Doma

Social Tradition

Betel nut, leaves, and a dash of lime make up this traditional snack often enjoyed during social gatherings, embodying Bhutan's sense of hospitality.

04

Momos

Popular Snack

Steamed or fried dumplings filled with meat or vegetables are a popular snack and meal option, reflecting Bhutan's proximity to Tibetan cuisine.

05

Phaksha Paa

Hearty Dish

Pork cooked with spicy red chilies and radishes creates a hearty dish that speaks to Bhutan's mountainous terrain and agricultural practices.

06

Suja

Butter Tea

Butter tea, a common drink in Bhutan, is made with tea leaves, butter, and salt. It provides warmth in the cold Himalayan climate.

Ready to taste Bhutan for yourself? Every GOMO Adventures journey brings you face-to-face with authentic Bhutanese flavor — from farmhouse kitchens to mountain teahouses.
