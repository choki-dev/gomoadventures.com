Accommodations

Where You'll Rest Along the Journey

Plan Your Stay

A Range of Options for Every Traveler

Accommodation in Bhutan offers a diverse range that caters to various preferences and budgets. When choosing accommodation, consider your preferences, budget, and desired experience — Gomo Adventures can assist in selecting suitable options tailored to your needs.

01
Best for Cultural Immersion

Homestays & Farmhouses

Stay with local families and experience rural Bhutanese life firsthand.

  • Homestays provide an immersive experience, staying with local families.
  • Farmhouses offer a glimpse of rural life and local culture.
  • Both options offer insights into Bhutanese daily life, culture, and traditions.
  • Basic but comfortable accommodations with shared facilities and home-cooked meals.
02
Best for Comfort & Amenities

Hotels & Resorts

Upscale resorts provide retreats in picturesque settings with extensive amenities.

Comfortable with standard amenities — restaurant, Wi-Fi, and private bathrooms.
Enhanced comfort, spacious rooms, modern facilities, restaurants, fitness centers, and spa services.
Luxurious accommodations with spacious rooms, fine dining, spas, and stunning views.
03
Best for Value Travelers

Budget Hotels

Clean, comfortable, and cost-effective — ideal for travelers focused on exploring.

  • Clean rooms, private bathrooms, and basic furnishings at economical rates.
  • Conveniently located in towns and cities for easy access to attractions.
  • Fewer services, but helpful staff for local recommendations.
  • Simple on-site meals or nearby eateries, with comfort prioritized.

Good to Know

Adjust expectations for amenities and services. Suitable for travelers focused on exploring Bhutan's culture and attractions — arranged through licensed tour operators as part of travel packages.

Whether opting for immersive cultural stays, comfortable hotels, or more luxurious resorts, Bhutan's accommodation options cater to diverse travel styles. Accommodations are typically pre-arranged through licensed tour operators as part of your travel package — let Gomo Adventures help you choose the right fit.
